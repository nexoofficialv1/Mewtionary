# Mewtionary v2.6 Status

Implemented:
- offline guided Conversation Lab
- voice and typed conversation replies
- keyword coverage and missing-keyword feedback
- transcript and dialogue scoring
- conversation rewards and badges
- Homework Studio with five writing structures
- draft generation, review, SQLite save, copy and text export
- Smart Revision prioritising weak skills
- skill-wise revision results and daily reward
- Parent Report printable HTML, CSV and text export
- SQLite schema v4
- daily-plan integration
- two new engagement badges
- content validation and test sources

Limitations:
- Conversation Lab is scenario-based, not an unrestricted LLM chat
- writing review uses structural rules, not human-level essay judgement
- report export depends on the platform file-save dialog
- installed curriculum and question banks remain sample content
- Flutter, Unity and physical Android compilation were unavailable in this environment
