# Pronunciation Coach v2.7

The coach provides:
- class-filtered target words and sentences
- Teacher read-aloud
- syllable and phrase chunks
- focus sound notation
- mouth-position guidance
- minimal-pair examples
- speech-recognition transcript
- word-coverage and sequence score
- missing/extra-word feedback
- offline attempt history
- XP, coins, badge and certificate milestones

The current score is transcript-based. It does not analyse the raw waveform, phoneme timing, accent or voice quality with a dedicated acoustic model.
