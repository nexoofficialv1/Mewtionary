# Mewtionary 3D Teacher — Final Character Art Brief

## Personality
একজন বন্ধুসুলভ, শিক্ষিত, প্রাণবন্ত তরুণ adult male teacher। তাকে দেখে শিশু ভয় পাবে না; আবার অতিরিক্ত শিশুসুলভও মনে হবে না।

## Face
- বড় expressive brown eyes
- soft rounded face
- natural eyebrows
- round dark glasses
- neat short beard and moustache
- gentle smile
- clear facial silhouette
- 7 viseme mouth shapes
- separate upper/lower eyelids

## Hair
- rich brown swept hairstyle
- clear large shapes, no tiny strands
- subtle highlight
- animation-friendly front lock

## Clothing
- warm white rolled-sleeve shirt
- charcoal/brown waistcoat
- deep blue tie
- tan trousers
- polished brown shoes
- wristwatch optional

## Props
- closed and open book
- pointer
- chalk
- pencil
- lesson cards

## Proportion
- 5.5–6 heads tall
- slightly larger head and hands for readability
- long but soft limbs
- hands must support clear gestures
- shoes large enough for dance readability

## Production requirements
- Original design only; no tracing of reference images
- 25k–45k triangles for mobile hero character
- one 2048×2048 texture atlas or equivalent
- 55–70 bones maximum
- facial blendshapes for expressions and visemes
- LOD0/LOD1/LOD2
- baked toon lighting support
- Android target: stable 30/60 FPS
