# Conversation Lab v2.6

Conversation Lab is an offline, child-safe guided dialogue engine.

Each scenario contains:
- a real-life location
- partner prompts
- Bangla support
- model-answer guidance
- expected keywords
- branching to the next dialogue node
- voice or typed reply
- transcript and score
- XP and coin rewards

The engine does not connect the child to strangers and does not provide unrestricted internet chat. It evaluates approved scenario replies through keyword coverage and stored teaching hints.
