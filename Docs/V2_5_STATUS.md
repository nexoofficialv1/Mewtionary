# Mewtionary v2.5 Status

Implemented:
- three educational mini-game engines
- level-filtered game content
- Listening Lab with MCQ and dictation
- speech-recognition or typed dictation
- interactive story-adventure library
- bilingual pages and Teacher narration
- tappable story vocabulary
- comprehension quiz and moral
- XP, coins, levels and streak
- duplicate-reward protection
- badge cabinet and automatic badge unlock
- daily-plan integration for games and listening
- SQLite schema v3
- content validation and unit-test sources

Limitations:
- coins are offline achievement points only
- voice scoring remains transcript-based
- bundled game, listening and story content is sample content
- no Flutter/Unity/device compilation was available in this environment
