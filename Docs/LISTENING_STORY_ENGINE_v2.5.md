# Listening and Story Engine v2.5

## Listening Lab

Two exercise modes are supported:
- listen and choose
- speech/type dictation

Dictation uses the existing transcript comparison engine. It is not raw-audio phoneme analysis.

## Story Adventures

A story adventure contains:
- English and Bangla pages
- Teacher read-aloud state
- tappable vocabulary
- comprehension questions
- moral
- completion reward

Story page position and final comprehension result are stored offline.
