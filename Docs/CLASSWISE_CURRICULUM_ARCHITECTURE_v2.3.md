# Class-wise Curriculum Architecture v2.3

Levels:
- Early Learner
- Classes 1–9

Skills:
- Vocabulary
- Grammar
- Reading
- Listening
- Speaking
- Writing
- Story
- Translation

The engine supports prerequisites, locked lessons, mastery scores, attempts and spaced revision. Review intervals are 1, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days according to mastery and repetition.

The bundled files demonstrate the architecture at every level. They are not represented as a complete academically reviewed or board-approved curriculum.
