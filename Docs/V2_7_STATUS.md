# Mewtionary v2.7 Status

Implemented:
- structured Pronunciation Coach
- 12 class-linked pronunciation exercises
- syllable chunks, sound focus, mouth tips and minimal pairs
- speech transcript scoring and attempt history
- five class-wise mock exam papers
- MCQ, fill blank, rearrange and short-answer engines
- timed exam, auto-submit, marks, skill score and answer review
- automatic seven-day study plan
- custom study tasks and completion rewards
- five printable certificate milestones
- parent report counts for pronunciation, mock exams and study tasks
- three new reward badges
- SQLite schema v5
- daily-plan integration
- content validator and unit-test sources

Limitations:
- pronunciation scoring is transcript-based, not phoneme-acoustic analysis
- short-answer marking is rule/key-idea based
- study planner does not yet schedule Android notifications
- certificates are app achievement documents only
- bundled lessons and exams are sample content
- Flutter, Unity and physical Android compilation were unavailable
